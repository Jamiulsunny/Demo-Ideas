# 60–90 second AresGrid video storyboard

## 0–8 s — The problem
Wide cinematic shot of a lone astronaut crossing a dusty Martian plain.
Voiceover: “On Mars, every kilometer is a resource decision. AresGrid turns scattered mission data into one Marswalk plan.”

## 8–20 s — Visor Mode
Cut to first-person visor HUD.
Show compass, waypoint arrow, oxygen, power, temperature and GO status.
Voiceover: “The astronaut sees the route in context: heading, distance, resources and hazards without leaving the field of view.”

## 20–35 s — Planner Mode
Hard cut to desktop mission-control UI.
Zoom into Jezero Crater.
Toggle terrain, water/ice, radiation, science and landmarks.
Voiceover: “Back at mission control, orbital and surface observations become selectable layers.”

## 35–50 s — Route intelligence
Select Safest, then Science-rich.
Show the route changing and dashboard metrics updating.
Voiceover: “A* searches the cost grid. The objective can change from safety to speed, science or resources.”

## 50–65 s — Hazard reroute
Enable a red radiation/terrain field.
Show route moving around the high-cost region.
Voiceover: “When a hazard appears, the route can be recomputed instead of forcing the crew through it.”

## 65–78 s — Science stops
Show automatic science-stop cards and a highlighted delta target.
Voiceover: “The system also protects science time by suggesting high-value stops along the walk.”

## 78–90 s — Closing
Return to the astronaut visor.
GO status, waypoint arrow and route timeline fill.
Voiceover: “AresGrid: Google Maps for Mars — designed to help a human explorer go safer, faster and more scientifically informed.”
